Ascentetik (UE5)

Prototype "Ascentetik" is a single-player platformer game where player navigate through brutalistic city with his parkour skills. The game is designed to test and research the mechanics of parkour movement in a 3D environment of Unreal Engine 5.
Created in Unreal Engine 5 using Blueprints and C++.
Project includes number of assets and plugins from Fab, intended for animation and blockout graphics.
